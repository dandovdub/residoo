Disclosed gap in bench/RESULTS.md: the benchmark corpus's private-key plants (`bench/corpus/generate.js`) use random-base64 bodies rather than structurally valid PEM/OpenSSH key material. TruffleHog's PrivateKey detector validates key structure before reporting, so it correctly does not flag our plants even though every other tested tool does (they marker-match on the `-----BEGIN...KEY-----` header). This unfairly penalizes any structure-validating scanner, TruffleHog included, and was left unfixed in v1 specifically to preserve corpus determinism across the v1 benchmark run.

The fix: generate real, syntactically valid (but obviously fake/throwaway, generated fresh, never reused anywhere) PEM or OpenSSH private key bodies for these plants, using Node's built-in `crypto` (no new dependency). Must keep the corpus generator deterministic (seeded) and regenerate `bench/corpus/truth.json` accordingly. See `bench/corpus/README.md` and `bench/README.md`'s methodology section before starting, the fairness bar for this benchmark is high and any corpus change should be defensible to a hostile reader.
